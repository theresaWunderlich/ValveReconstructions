Quantifying valve tissue in a congenital heart disease model

Altered blood flow in Tetralogy of Fallot reduced the cellular fraction of the pulmonary valve while leaving the aortic valve unchanged — a valve-specific pattern consistent with a flow-driven effect. The reconstructions below are the 3D volumes behind that result.

How it was made

ToF was induced in chick embryos with cyclopamine and confirmed in vivo by ultrasound of aortic blood flow. One control and one ToF heart were prepared for volume electron microscopy, sampling the pulmonary and aortic valves.

Tissue was split into cellular and extracellular classes with a two-class U-Net trained on hand-traced ground-truth slices. Nuclei were segmented separately and carved out of the cellular class, so the verified cellular/matrix split stays fixed. Volume fractions are voxel-count ratios, independent of the voxel-size calibration.
